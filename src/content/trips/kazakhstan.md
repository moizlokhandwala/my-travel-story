## Wheels up from Sharjah, cash in hand

We flew out of Sharjah airport with up to 20,000 KZT in local currency and 1,500 USD in cash.

## Day 1 · 3 October: Hello, Almaty

### Touchdown, then getting connected

We landed in Almaty around 4 pm. At the airport we bought a local SIM for around 6,000 KZT. It came with a monthly package of unlimited internet. You need to show your passport to get one. Alternatively, we also had a Holafly eSIM, which works well too.

### Our driver-guide was already waiting

We had hired a local guide and driver through a travel company in the UAE, who charged us 4,000 AED. He was waiting to pick us up at the airport.

### Home for three nights: the Ramada Almaty

We booked our hotels on our own. In Almaty we stayed at the Ramada Almaty from 3 to 6 October, in a suite with a bedroom and a living room with a sofa bed. We reached the hotel around 5:30 pm. It was a decent hotel and the suite was nice.

The hotel is a bit far from places you might want to visit on your own, like Arbat Street, but that was fine.

### First night out: an Indian dinner and Arbat Street

We freshened up and, around 7:30 pm, walked about seven minutes to a nearby Indian restaurant called Namaste India. The food was just okay.

After that we went back to the hotel and then headed out to Arbat Street. There was a taxi driver's number at the hotel reception, so we messaged him on WhatsApp and he came to pick us up within five minutes. He dropped us at Arbat Street, and we explored the area. When we were done, we messaged him again and he came back to get us. The round trip cost 6,000 KZT.

That was the end of Day 1.

## First impressions of Almaty

### Many cultures in one city

We found people from many different cultures there. The Indian restaurant had Pakistani staff, and the usher who took our luggage was from Afghanistan.

### Kazakh, Russian and a translator app

People there speak Kazakh and Russian. A translator app was a real help for us.

### A city full of parks

Almaty has a lot of parks. We could not visit any of them, but we saw plenty while passing by.

## Tips from the road (so far)

- **Local SIM:** around 6,000 KZT at the Almaty airport, with unlimited internet for a month. Bring your passport. A Holafly eSIM is a good alternative.
- **Language:** people speak Kazakh and Russian, so keep a translator app on your phone. It was a real help for us.
- **Pack a portable bidet spray:** many toilets do not have one.
- **Hotel location:** the Ramada Almaty is a bit far from sights like Arbat Street, so you will want a taxi to get around.
- **Taxis:** ask hotel reception for a driver's number and message him on WhatsApp. Ours arrived within five minutes, and the round trip to Arbat Street cost 6,000 KZT.
